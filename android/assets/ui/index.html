Auto Apply

Apply to jobs while you live your life

Answer a few questions once. Then the app applies to LinkedIn Easy Apply jobs that fit your CV, in the background, and tells you what it sent.

  • Create your account (it connects the AI that reads jobs and fills forms)
  • Add your CV
  • Answer the questions forms ask most, like your expected salary
  • Pick the jobs to look for
  • Sign in to LinkedIn

Takes about 3 minutes.

Create your account

Your account connects the AI that reads jobs and fills in forms. No AI key needed.

Signed in as

Ask whoever runs your Auto Apply server.

At least 8 characters.

Add your CV

A PDF with real text (not a scanned photo). The AI uses it to choose jobs and answer questions.

Answers forms ask for

Most Easy Apply forms ask these. Answer once and the app fills them in every form.

Which jobs?

Job titles to search for on LinkedIn. The app applies only where the job fits your CV.

Job titles

A country or a city.

Sign in to LinkedIn

LinkedIn only takes Easy Apply applications from a signed-in account, so this step is needed once. The app remembers it.

Signed in to LinkedIn

Use your email and password. "Sign in with Google" does not work inside apps.
